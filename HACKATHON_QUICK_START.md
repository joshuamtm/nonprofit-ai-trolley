## 💡 Pro Tips for a Winning Submission

### Video
- ✅ **Energy**: Speak with enthusiasm—you're excited about this!
- ✅ **Show, don't tell**: Less talking, more demo
- ✅ **URL at the end**: Make it big and hold for 5+ seconds
- ✅ **Subtitles**: Add them (accessibility + clarity)
- ✅ **Music**: Optional but nice (royalty-free, low volume)

### PDFs
- ✅ **Visuals**: Include diagrams, screenshots, charts
- ✅ **White space**: Don't cram text—make it scannable
- ✅ **Bold key points**: Help judges find important info
- ✅ **Page numbers**: Professional touch
- ✅ **Proofread**: No typos (ask someone to review)

### Presentation
- ✅ **Tell a story**: Problem → Solution → Impact
- ✅ **Real examples**: Use case studies (even hypothetical ones)
- ✅ **Metrics**: 62% performance boost, 5-minute assessment, etc.
- ✅ **Passion**: Why does THIS problem matter to YOU?

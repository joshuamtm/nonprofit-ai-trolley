4. Review & Results (12 seconds)
   - Show the trolley animation
   - Display the three paths side-by-side
   - Highlight personalized recommendations
   - Click "Download PDF Report"
   - Show brief glimpse of PDF opening

>
> Then they get a personalized analysis showing three paths: implement AI, maintain the status quo, or implement with safeguards. Each path includes specific benefits, risks, and actionable recommendations tailored to their situation.

**On-screen text/callouts**:
- "5-Minute Assessment"

## 🎥 PRODUCTION TIPS

### Recording Options

**Option A: Screen Recording + Voiceover** (Easiest)
1. Use Loom, QuickTime, or OBS to record screen
2. Record voiceover separately or live
3. Edit in iMovie, DaVinci Resolve (free), or CapCut

**Option B: Picture-in-Picture** (More engaging)
1. Record yourself talking (phone/webcam)
2. Record screen separately
3. Combine with picture-in-picture effect
4. You appear in corner during demo

**Option C: Talking Head + B-Roll** (Most professional)
1. Record yourself presenting (well-lit, good audio)
2. Screen recordings as B-roll during technical parts
3. Cut between face and screen strategically

### Recording Checklist
- [ ] Good lighting (natural light or lamp in front)
- [ ] Clear audio (use headphones mic or external mic)
- [ ] Quiet environment (no background noise)
- [ ] Browser in full-screen or clean state (no distracting tabs)
- [ ] Cursor movements are smooth and deliberate
- [ ] Text on screen is readable (large fonts)
- [ ] Speak clearly and with energy (smile!)

### Editing Checklist
- [ ] Keep total time under 2 minutes (ideally 90 seconds)
- [ ] Add captions/subtitles (accessibility + better comprehension)
- [ ] Background music (optional, low volume, royalty-free)
- [ ] Zoom in on important UI elements during demo
- [ ] Speed up boring parts (form filling) to 1.5-2x
- [ ] Add transitions between sections (simple cuts are fine)
- [ ] Include URL at the end (hold for 5+ seconds)
- [ ] Export in 1080p MP4

### Energy & Delivery
- **Enthusiasm**: Sound excited about your project!
- **Pace**: Speak slightly faster than normal conversation
- **Clarity**: Enunciate technical terms clearly
- **Pauses**: Brief pauses for emphasis (3-4 throughout)
- **Smile**: Even in voiceover, smiling affects your tone

## 🎤 VOICE RECORDING TIPS

### Before Recording
- [ ] Drink room-temperature water (not cold)
- [ ] Do a few vocal warm-ups (humming, lip trills)
- [ ] Read script aloud 3-4 times to get comfortable
- [ ] Mark where to pause for emphasis
- [ ] Clear your throat and do a test recording

### During Recording
- [ ] Stand up (better breath support and energy)
- [ ] Smile while speaking (it changes your tone positively)
- [ ] Use hand gestures even if not on camera (more natural energy)
- [ ] Record each section separately (easier to re-do if needed)
- [ ] Take breaks every 10 minutes to rest your voice

### Audio Quality
- **Good**: iPhone/headphone mic close to your mouth
- **Better**: External USB microphone (Blue Yeti, etc.)
- **Best**: Lavalier mic clipped to your shirt

- **Environment**: Closet or small room with soft materials (absorbs echo)
- **Avoid**: Kitchens, bathrooms (lots of reverb)

## 💡 INSPIRATION & EXAMPLES

### What Makes a Great Hackathon Demo Video?

1. **Hook immediately** (first 5 seconds matter most)
2. **Show, don't just tell** (live demo beats talking about features)
3. **Clear problem statement** (viewers need to understand the "why")
4. **Energy and passion** (judges want to see you care)
5. **Technical credibility** (mention the stack, show code briefly)
6. **Real impact** (not hypothetical—actual use cases)
7. **Clear CTA** (what do you want judges to do? Try it!)

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- ❌ Too much talking, not enough showing
- ❌ Monotone voice / reading script robotically
- ❌ Poor audio quality (instant credibility loss)
- ❌ Showing bugs or errors (edit them out!)
- ❌ Going over 2 minutes (judges will stop watching)
- ❌ Not showing the URL (how will judges find it?)
- ❌ Apologizing or being self-deprecating
